Ambition

To be socially and culturally relevant to a group of people who care about music — a local scene, say, or a fanbase somewhere else. A fair measure might be people showing up at gigs, and monthly listeners on streaming. But output and growth are a consequence, not a condition for existing.

Adult life keeps its own schedule. So the band’s energy has to be spent strategically: content worth making, gigs that give something back, audiences that actually consume music. Festivals, Bolacha Discos, rooms of that kind.

Writing and producing have to be good moments, rewarded by the experience itself, whatever the outcome.

First goal

Build an original repertoire and start playing live.

The plan

01

One session only

Record all 12 tracks at once, live — bass, drums and guitar playing together.

02

Then, four songs at a time

Each block runs the same path, in EP order:

  • Final tracking — vocals and effects over the base
  • Mixing and mastering
  • Visuals for all four — visualiser, lyric video, music video or cover art
  • Registration and distribution — ISRC and submission to the distributor
  • Release — with a Spotify pitch on one focus track
03

All in parallel

Nobody waits for anybody. While one block is being finished, the next is already queued and the band keeps playing.

=

In the end

Three closed EPs of four tracks. No standalone singles, all within 2027.
Then: a consolidated playlist on streaming + the K7 tape with all twelve.

Name in text

Three settled forms, each with its own place. They are not interchangeable: swapping one for another in the wrong context weakens recognition of the name.

Oil Can No. 9

Full name

First mention in any text, credits, contracts, release metadata and distributor forms. Always with the period and the space: No. 9.

OCN9

Short form

Tight spaces, internal use and merch: filenames, tab titles, labels, document headers. It never opens a text — it only appears after the full name.

@oilcanno9

Handle

Social media, email and any username. Always lowercase, no period, no space, no separator.

Do not write

  • Oil Can #9  ·  Oil Can nº 9  ·  Oil Can No.9 (no space)
  • OilCan No. 9  ·  Oilcan No. 9  ·  OIL CAN NO. 9 (all caps, outside a heading)
  • OCN 9  ·  OCN-9  ·  ocn9 (the short form is always caps)
  • @oil_can_no9  ·  @OilCanNo9  ·  @ocn9

Neutral — cool greys

On a dark ground, the brand blue and red fall short of the minimum reading contrast (WCAG AA). In dark theme the interface uses the 300 step of those scales for type and icons — the brand colour stays reserved for surfaces and printed pieces.

Symbol

The can. It works alone where the name is already established or already sits nearby: social avatars, seals, bass drum, stickers, video watermark. It is never the brand’s first introduction to a new audience.

On a dark or coloured ground, always use the negative. The two-colour version exists on white only — the red of the No. 9 does not separate from black or from blue.

Files: symbol-1color.svg · symbol-2colors.svg · symbol-negative.svg

Coexistence

Logo and symbol share the same visual alphabet, but they never appear together in the same visual field. It is the Volkswagen rule: either the VW roundel, or "Das Auto" — never the two in one lockup. On a two-sided piece the rule holds per face: a tee with the logo on the chest and the symbol on the back is correct, because the two are never seen at once.
